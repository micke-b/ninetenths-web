About

Built by engineers who drive.

Nine Tenths Engineering AB is a Swedish engineering company. We build the things we want on track ourselves, and we test them there.

The name comes from motorsport: driving at nine tenths means pushing hard while staying in control. It is also a reminder that there is always a last tenth left to find, in a lap time or in a product.
